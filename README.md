# Hi, I'm Lynsha 👋

**Physics → Applied Mathematics → Data Science & AI**

I work at the intersection of **applied mathematics, machine learning, and data science**, with a focus on turning data and mathematical reasoning into practical, decision-ready solutions.

My interests include deep learning, generative models, optimal transport, and the use of AI to solve real problems in industry and society.

I hold an **MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Data Science)** from AIMS Rwanda, and a **BSc in Fundamental Physics** and a **Master 1 in Materials Science** from the University of Yaoundé I.

## 🔬 Research

My MSc thesis explored **deep learning for the design of solid polymer electrolytes**, combining a physics-informed graph neural network that predicts ionic conductivity with a conditional variational autoencoder that generates candidate polymer structures.

I like understanding **why a model works**, and building physical or mathematical structure into it, rather than treating it as a black box.
